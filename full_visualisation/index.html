Malaysia Crime Levels Across States

Selected Year: —

Since 2016, urban states such as Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya and Selangor record higher crime intensity than rural states. Crime rates generally decline over time, reflecting enforcement and community initiatives.

Reported Crimes Across Years

Property crime dominates annually. A sharp drop in 2020 coincides with COVID-19 mobility restrictions, followed by stabilisation.

Composition of Crime Types

Theft-related offences form the largest share. Assault is smaller; murder/rape are the smallest categories.

Crime Rate vs Poverty Across Years

Poverty headcount ratio declined while crime rates peaked in 2016 then fell (notably in 2020), indicating improving conditions and movement restrictions.

Crime vs Unemployment by Region

No strong linear link between unemployment and crime. Urban density (KL, Selangor) aligns more with higher rates than unemployment alone.

Distribution of Crime Rates Across Income Groups

High-income group shows the widest spread (outliers like KL), medium-income states have the highest median, and low-income states show a lower central tendency.
