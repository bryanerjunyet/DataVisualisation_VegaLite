Malaysia Crime Levels Across States

Selected Year: —

Since 2016, urban states such as Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya and Selangor record higher crime intensity than rural states. Crime rates generally decline over time, reflecting nationwide enforcement efforts and community policing initiatives have been effective in driving down crime over time.

Reported Crimes Across Years

Property crime dominates annually. A sharp drop in 2020 coincides with COVID-19 mobility restrictions, followed by stabilisation.

Composition of Crime Types

Theft crimes dominate the largest share. Assault murder and rape are the smallest categories throughout the years.

Crime Rate vs Poverty Rate Across Years

Both crime rate and poverty rate decline parallelly, suggesting poverty reduction may be associated with lower crime.

Crime vs Unemployment Rates by Region

No strong relationship. Central Malaysia has the highest crimes despite moderate unemployment, while East Malaysia has higher unemployment but only moderate crime.

Distribution of Crime Rates Across Income Groups

Crime index rises sharply with income group. High-income group shows the widest spread of crime rates, while medium-income group clusters tightly and low-income group show the lowest levels and minimal variation.
