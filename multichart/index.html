Malaysia Crime Patterns across All States

Malaysia Crime Levels

Since 2016, urban states such as Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya and Selangor consistently record higher crime levels compared to rural states. These states serve as major economic and transportation hubs, attracting large populations and increased social activity, which tend to correlate with higher crime incidence. Throughout the years, the trend is encouraging as the intensity of crime noticeably declines across nearly all states, including previously high-crime states like Putrajaya and Selangor, which strongly indicates nationwide enforcement efforts and community policing initiatives have been effective in driving down crime over time.

Assault vs Property Crimes

Since 2016, property crimes overwhelmingly dominate total reported cases every year, consistently making up the largest share of offences nationwide. Prior to major crime reform initiatives, a notable downward trend in overall crime after 2016, especially a sharp drop in 2020 coincides with COVID-19 movement restrictions, which limited public interaction and mobility, leading a temporary but pronounced fall in both property and assault crimes.
